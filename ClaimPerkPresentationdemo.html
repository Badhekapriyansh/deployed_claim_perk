01 · The Problem

Your discount is real. It's just scattered across six different apps.

A bank offer sits in one app, a coupon code in a browser tab, a cashback percentage in another, a referral reward somewhere else entirely. Almost nobody applies all of them — most shoppers just take whichever one they remember.

Bank / UPI offer10% off, buried in your card app
Coupon codeCopied, then forgotten at checkout
Cashback appNeeds its own separate click-through
Referral rewardSitting unused in a rewards wallet
02 · The Solution

Claim Perks reads every offer for you — then does the math.

It aggregates coupons, cashback, referral rewards, affiliate deals, and bank/UPI offers across Amazon, Flipkart, Myntra, Paytm, PhonePe and CRED, then calculates the final payable price before you buy.
